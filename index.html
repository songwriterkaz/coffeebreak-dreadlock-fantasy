Colour the dreads

Pick the Colour tool, then tap or swipe over single dreads to colour them one by one. If a dread is selected, colour changes go straight onto it.

Beads and extras

Choose an extra, then tap a dread to thread it on. It moves with the dread. Drag any extra to slide it somewhere else.

Fantasy magic

Brush

Draw a dread makes a real dread you can colour, bend and bead. Paint, Glow, Glitter and Dots draw patterns and colour straight onto the picture.

Add my photo

Your photo replaces the drawn face. Tap Move photo, then drag it so your head sits under the dreads. Use the sliders to size and turn it. Your photo stays on this device and is never uploaded.

Dreadlock Fantasy

Design your dream dreads. Pick a style, colour every dread your way, drag and bend them, draw your own, then thread on beads, cuffs, wraps, shells, charms, string, flowers and crystals. Add glow and glitter for some fantasy magic, then save your picture.

Move tap to pick, drag to bend or move. Colour tap or swipe dreads. Draw paint or draw new dreads. Add bead tap a dread to add the chosen extra. Erase tap or swipe to remove.
